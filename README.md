I'm a master's student in Computer Vision at **Carnegie Mellon University**, doing research with Prof. **Katerina Fragkiadaki**. Before CMU, I was a student researcher at the **Allen Institute for AI (Ai2)** and the University of Washington, where I worked on models that understand and predict how things move in 3D.
